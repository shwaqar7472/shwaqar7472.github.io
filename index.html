Haven

Your life. Your space. By SH Waqar.

Haven is a private, offline-first life organiser for Android. Tasks, money, habits, documents, family dates and more live in one app, encrypted on your own phone. There's no account to create and no server that holds your data.

✅ Tasks & habitsLists, reminders, repeats, streaks and a weekly review.
💰 FinancesWallets, budgets, debts, savings goals and salary-to-salary months, in your currency.
📄 Documents & VaultScan papers and IDs, get expiry reminders, keep passwords and cards private.
👪 Family & FirstsBirthdays, anniversaries, gift ideas and life’s first moments.
🛒 Shopping & BelongingsLists that pay through Finances, a wardrobe and outfit ideas.
❤️ Health & NotesWeight, fasting, notes and checklists.

Private by design

Everything is stored in an encrypted database on your phone and protected by your PIN or fingerprint. If you choose, encrypted backups go to a hidden app folder in your own Google Drive, locked with a password only you know. Haven has no ads and no tracking. Read the privacy policy.

Languages

English, اردو, العربية, हिन्दी, Español and Français, with 28 currencies.
