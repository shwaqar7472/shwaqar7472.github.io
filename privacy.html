Privacy policy

Haven for Android, by SH Waqar · Last updated 24 November 2026

In short: your data stays on your phone, encrypted. The developer never receives it. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or trackers. Data only leaves your phone when you choose to, such as an encrypted backup to your own Google Drive or a file you share.

1. What Haven stores, and where

Everything you enter (tasks, money, habits, notes, documents, photos, family details, health entries and settings) is stored only on your phone, in a database encrypted with SQLCipher. Its key is kept in Android’s secure storage. Your PIN and fingerprint setting also stay on the phone. Haven has no server and no user accounts.

2. Google Drive backups (optional)

If you tap “Connect Google Drive”, you sign in with Google and Haven asks for one permission, drive.appdata. This lets Haven use only its own hidden app folder in your Drive: it cannot see, open or change any of your other files. Haven reads your Google account’s email address only to show which account is connected.

The backup files are encrypted on your phone with a backup password that only you know before they are uploaded, so neither Google nor the developer can read them. Haven keeps your newest 14 backups there. You can disconnect at any time in Settings → Backups, or remove access in your Google Account’s security settings.

Haven’s use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Data from Google is used only to store and restore your own backups, is never transferred to anyone else, and is not used for advertising.

4. Phone permissions

Haven asks only when a feature needs it:

  • Camera: to scan documents and barcodes and take photos.
  • Contacts: only when you choose “Add from contacts”; only the people you pick are copied in.
  • Notifications and exact alarms: for reminders you set.
  • Fingerprint: to unlock Haven, if you turn it on.

None of these send anything off your phone.

5. Sharing and exports

When you share a document, export a spreadsheet or save a backup file, it goes only where you send it. Exported spreadsheets are not encrypted. Shared documents can be protected with a password you set.

6. Error reports

If Haven hits an error, it is recorded in a private log on your phone. Nothing is sent unless you choose to share a report.

7. Children

Haven is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from anyone.

8. Your control

Your data is yours. You can edit or delete anything in the app, disconnect Google Drive, delete Drive backups by removing Haven’s access in your Google Account, or uninstall the app to remove everything stored on the phone.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
